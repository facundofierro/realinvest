Vest logo proposals

Four directions for the vest-realestate.com brand mark. Each row shows the lockup on light, the lockup on dark, and the app icon down to favicon size. All four keep the current purple and fuchsia palette and fix the subtitle to “Real Estate”.

1 · Fold

An evolution of today's logo. The two-tone V stays as the first letter of the name, but the right stroke now rises above the left, so the letter reads as a folded facade and as an upward trend. Lowest risk: existing users will recognise it.

2 · Skyline

Two buildings of different heights whose inner walls lean apart. The V is the sky between them. The most literal real-estate reading, with an uppercase, architectural wordmark. The windows drop out at favicon size.

3 · Pin

A rounded V with a dot above it. It reads as a map pin marking a place, and as a person with raised arms: the buyer. The friendliest of the four, and the simplest shape, so it holds up best at 16 pixels.

4 · Floors

A bold V sliced into horizontal bands, like the floors of a building or rows in an inventory of units. The gradient runs from light fuchsia at the top to deep purple at the base. The most product-specific idea: it speaks to managing units, which is what developers buy.
