## Deviations and things to know

- **Building illustration**: windows are drawn from a regular 5-column grid instead of the artboard's irregular offsets, so the unit colours differ slightly from the design.
- **Hero**: the unit rows get right padding at `sm+` so the status badges are not covered by the overlapping phone mock; the phone is hidden below `sm`.
- **Honeypot**: the schema allows the hidden field to be filled and the API silently drops those submissions, so a browser autofill cannot trap a real user behind an invisible error.
- **Form inputs** are native elements styled to the design rather than the `@repo/ui` `Input`/`Form` components, whose styling differs from design C.
- **Dark mode** was skipped (optional in the plan).
- **Launch step 2 and "Desde el exterior"** keep the longer copy-doc text from Phase 2 rather than the shorter artboard wording.
