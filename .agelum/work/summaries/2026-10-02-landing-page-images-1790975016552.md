## Result

12 images were generated with `fal-ai/nano-banana-pro` (genmedia), and 9 SVG illustration slots in `apps/landing` were replaced with them. An OG/Twitter image was added. None of the images contain text; all copy stays in HTML.

The 3 phone/UI mocks (hero phone, buyer-portal phone, investor app mock) are **not replaced**. Per the plan's rule ("if a screen can't be captured with safe sample data, ask the user"), they stay as SVG pending a decision (see "Open decision").

## Open decision (needs the user)

The plan called for real wallet screenshots in the 3 phone slots. Screens were captured from `apps/wallet` as the seeded `demo-user`: `/`, `/invest`, `/assets`, `/project/torre-libertador-8000`, and `/units`. None are usable:
- Every screen shows tokens, ROI/TIR %, "Renta fija", P&L or "Tokens de propiedad", all forbidden by the landing copy rules (`es.ts:1-6`).
- There is no buyer-portal screen (construction progress, installments, documents) to capture.

Options:
1. Keep the current SVG mocks. This is the current state, and they already match the landing copy.
2. Build a dedicated, copy-safe demo screen or route in the wallet and capture that.
3. Hide the offending elements with injected CSS before capturing.

The capture script is kept at `.imagegen/shots.mjs` (run it from `apps/e2e`).
